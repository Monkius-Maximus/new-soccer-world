# ADR-0005 — Version 1.0 scope

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-08-08

## Decision

Version 1.0 centers on an offline football game: quick match, league/cup competition, club career, tactics, transfers/contracts, progression/aging, basic scouting/training and player/club editing with kit/crest customization constrained by the proven render pipeline.

Life World, explorable cities, social smartphone systems, family/relationship simulation, Player Career, online/crossplay, FUT-like live-service economy and a complete stadium editor are not 1.0 requirements.

## Consequences

Foundation and MATCH can prioritize the football simulation. Deferred systems may build on the same persistent world later without blocking a coherent first commercial game.
